## First comment (post this yourself, right after launching)

```
Hi Product Hunt 👋

CurrentWire answers one question: what are the 100 stories actually moving
in the US and Canada right now, and who reported them first?

It is fully automated. There is no human editorial desk and it produces no
original reporting. What it does is cluster coverage of the same event from
many publishers into a single story page, then rank the clusters by
freshness, how broadly the story is being covered, source authority and
momentum. Every story links back to the publishers that did the work, and
the ranking method is published at /methodology rather than being a black
box.

Two things I care about and would like feedback on:

- Attribution. Aggregators have a bad reputation for burying the original
  reporter. Every cluster names its sources and links out. Tell me if you
  find a story where that reads unfairly to the publisher.
- Story pages are permanent. A URL from today still resolves in a year, so
  a story you cite does not rot.

It is free, no account needed, and there is an RSS feed at
https://currentwire.us/rss

Happy to answer anything about the ranking pipeline or the dedup.
```
