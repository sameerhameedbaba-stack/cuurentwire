### A hypothesis tested and killed the same run

Production answers a request carrying **Googlebot's user agent** in 7–10
seconds where the same URL answers a browser UA in 0.14 s — on a
`X-Vercel-Cache: HIT` of static `/robots.txt`, from the same edge node, with
the entire delay in TTFB. It fires only on the exact-case string `Googlebot`
(`googlebot`, `bingbot`, `GPTBot`, `Google-InspectionTool`, `curl` and an
empty UA are all ~0.13 s), and nothing in this repo does it — there is no
`middleware.ts` and no user-agent branch in `app/`, `lib/` or `vercel.json`.

That is a complete-looking answer: a crawler that waits ten seconds for
`robots.txt` backs off hard.

**The control refuted it.** The same probe against three unrelated
Vercel-hosted sites:

| host | browser UA | Googlebot UA |
|---|---|---|
| currentwire.us | 0.23 s | 10.18 s |
| vercel.com | 0.24 s | 10.20 s |
| nextjs.org | 0.72 s | 10.19 s |
| resend.com | 0.51 s | 10.16 s |

A ~10.2 s hold on Vercel's own marketing site is platform behaviour —
reverse-DNS verification of a *claimed* Googlebot arriving from a residential
IP. Real Googlebot fetches from verified Google ranges and is not this client.
**The site is not tarpitting Googlebot; this is not the cause; do not re-open
it.** Memory note:
`seo/MEMORY/2026-09-09-the-platform-tarpits-a-spoofed-crawler.md`.

### One asymmetry that is worth the owner's two minutes

Google last read `archive-sitemap.xml` around Sep 3–4, but `news-sitemap.xml`
and `sitemap.xml` not since Aug 24. The one it read is the one that was newly
**submitted** on Sep 4. So a fresh submission still pulls a fetch out of
Google even during the blackout. Re-submitting the two unread sitemaps is a
falsifiable experiment with a monitor already pointed at the answer — see
*Needed from the owner*. This loop cannot do it: every GSC script here holds
`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/webmasters.readonly`, and `sitemaps.submit`
needs the write scope, which needs a new owner consent.

## 2. Shipped: `generalOrphanPct` is instrumented, and measured over the whole corpus

The first unblocked item on the 09-04 engineering queue.

`general` is the classifier's abstention bucket and `/general` is
`noindex, nofollow` on purpose, so a story routed there appears on **no
indexable category page at all** — it loses the listing, the internal-link
path and the topical grouping (BACKLOG item 6). Two readings of that share
exist in this repo, 3 of 22 (14%, 08-25) and 9 of 40 (23%, 08-26), and neither
could settle whether it was a regression: at n=22 the observation and the
benchmark's designed 34.8% are about one standard error apart.

`scripts/general-orphan-rate.mjs` + `general-orphan-lib.mjs` read the **whole**
news-sitemap corpus and take `articleSection` from each story page's
NewsArticle JSON-LD — every block `JSON.parse`d, never regexed, because a
greedy attribute regex mis-measured two section descriptions on 09-08 and this
figure is meant to decide whether the classifier gets changed.

**Measured live 2026-09-08 22:54 UTC:**

    generalOrphanPct: 22.7% — 162 general of 715 story pages read (0 unread)

    general    162  22.7%  <- orphan bucket, noindex
    politics   118  16.5%
    world       93  13%
    business    78  10.9%
    technology  62   8.7%
    sports      61   8.5%
    culture     47   6.6%
    health      35   4.9%
    science     35   4.9%
    climate     24   3.4%

Zero sampling error: the denominator is the corpus. 22.7% is **below** the
benchmark's designed 34.8%, which reproduces the 08-26 conclusion on real
evidence instead of a 40-story sample — the classifier is operating to its
precision-over-recall contract and this is not a regression to chase. Series
in `data/general-orphan-history.json`.

It is an **instrument, not a gate**: no threshold, and it never fails on a
high rate. A gate that fires on the designed value hides the bug it was built
to find. The only failure it reports is a failure to measure.

30 unit tests. **Negative control run before commit**: with the section fold
made case-sensitive and the read-ratio floor removed, 9 of the 30 failed;
restored, all 30 pass.

### Two defects in the instrument, found by running it rather than reading it

- The **first live run read 153 of 727** and said only that — with no way to
  tell a cold cache from a rate limit from a broken parser. A warm re-run read
  727 of 727, so the cause was cold ISR entries rendering at the origin under
  the 30 s ceiling. It now tallies *why* each unread page was unread.
- It printed **"168 of 727 story pages read"** when 727 were read and 168 were
  general: the count and the denominator were readable as each other. Fixed,
  and a test now asserts the exact wording.

Both are the reason the run refused to publish a rate on the first attempt.
The "NOT MEASURED" path did its job.

**Boundary check:** this change touches no caching, TTL, persistence window or
deploy-skip mechanism. It adds one read-only script, its pure lib, its tests,
and one data file. **Cost note:** a full sweep is ~715 cached GETs; on a cold
corpus some of those force an ISR re-render, which is billed. `seo-health.mjs`
already sweeps the same corpus daily, so this roughly doubles that one sweep —
~0.4% of the ~165K ISR writes/day recorded on 09-03.

deployment carries the env vars and is the route in); the measurement above is
recorded so it is not re-taken.
