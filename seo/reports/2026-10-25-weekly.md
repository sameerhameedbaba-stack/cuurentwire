## What moved

- **New evidence this run, from a source neither this cloud sandbox's proxy nor its egress path could have produced: GitHub's own Actions runners.** The `bluesky-post.yml` workflow runs on GitHub-hosted runners (Azure, no Anthropic proxy in the path) and makes a real outbound HTTPS request to `https://currentwire.us` from `scripts/bluesky-post.mjs`. Today it failed twice — 16:35 UTC (westus3) and 20:59 UTC (eastus) — both times with `ERR_TLS_CERT_ALTNAME_INVALID` against a `*.github.io` cert, the generic Pages fallback, not the custom-domain cert. That is strictly stronger evidence than anything this routine could gather via its own sandbox probes, because it rules out "proxy artifact" entirely.
- This **corrects** the 2026-10-21 weekly's "certificate rollout now looks COMPLETE" framing. It wasn't wrong about the 4 specific edge IPs this sandbox can reach directly (those still check out today) — it was incomplete: GitHub Pages' custom-domain cert has not propagated to every edge node 9 days after the 10-16 finding and 4 days after being called "done."
- This also gives a candidate explanation for why `bluesky-post.yml` has silently failed every run since whenever this cert gap opened, and so for why Bluesky has posted nothing through the whole outage — not just "the off-page routine is correctly staying off," but an actively broken scheduled job hitting the wrong cert, maybe adjacent to whatever is also causing the 503 split.
- Deploy pipeline stayed green all week (confirmed again today) — rules out a build/deploy regression as a contributor.
