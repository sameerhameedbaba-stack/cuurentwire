## 2. The failure was not a network blip — it was an infinite redirect loop

`TypeError: fetch failed` reads like a transport hiccup, and the same URL
answered **200 on three consecutive `curl` retries**, which made a blip look
confirmed. It was not. Node's `fetch` with `redirect: "follow"` throws that
exact bare string for a **redirect cycle** — the real cause,
`redirect count exceeded`, sits on `error.cause` and never reached the log.

Traced by hand:

```
/story/multiple-people-dead-including-kids-in-shooting-and-fire-at-montana-home-c73a14a645aa9
  -> 307 /story/multiple-people-killed-in-shooting-at-montana-home-also-set-on-fire-officials-sa-c73a14a645aa9
  -> 307 back to the first, indefinitely
```

Both slugs belong to the same cluster `c73a14a645aa9`. **This was live on
`/news-sitemap.xml`** — the feed Googlebot-News fetches within minutes of
publication — so a crawler following that entry reached no page at all.

**Blast radius, measured rather than assumed:** all 643 news-sitemap URLs
were chased hop by hop. **1 loop, 0 other problems.**

**Root cause, from cache headers:**

| URL | `Age` | `X-Vercel-Cache` | redirects to |
|---|---|---|---|
| slug A | **17,547 s (~4.9 h)** | HIT | slug B |
| slug B | 325 s (~5 min) | HIT | slug A |
| `/story/c73a14a645aa9` (fresh) | — | **MISS** | **slug A** |

The fresh render says canonical is **A**, so B was behaving correctly. A was
a **stale cached redirect frozen ~5 hours earlier**, from when the lead
headline made B canonical. `resolveStoryRequest` returns its redirect from
the live branch *before* any tagged data read, so the redirect response
carries no `NEWS_CACHE_TAG`, `revalidateTag` never invalidates it, and it
inherits the 30-day `/story/[slug]` TTL. A redirect can outlive the slug it
points at by weeks; pair it with a fresh one pointing back and the cycle is
permanent.

Headline flapping is ordinary, not exotic: the `mike-lindell-...` pair
swapped redirect direction between two health-check runs minutes apart in
this same session.

## 5. Backlog item 6 (`general` bucket) — reproduced, and its framing corrected

Yesterday's backlog said this outranks everything except an outage if
reproduced. It reproduced, so it was investigated properly — and the
investigation changed the conclusion in both directions.

- **The share is real and larger:** 40 story pages sampled through
  `/news-sitemap.xml`, read from `articleSection` — **9 of 40 (23%) are
  `General`**, tied with Sports as the largest section, against 14%
  yesterday. Nearly all nine are plainly misfiled (Artemis II livestreams, a
  European rights court ruling on Turkey, a Montreal airport death, two Dolly
  Parton pieces, a Buddhism explainer).
- **But it is NOT a regression.** `data/benchmark-history.json` records the
  designed rate as `realGeneralCount: 109` of 313 = **34.8%**. Re-running
  `classifyCategory` over the 313-story validated truth set reproduces the
  recorded benchmark exactly — **73.2% exact, 5.4% wrong-specific, 110
  general (35.1%)**. The live 23% is *below* design. Reported as such rather
  than as an alarm.
- **What is genuinely worth fixing, quantified:** of 110 general verdicts the
  truth set calls only 43 genuinely general — **67 of 313 (21%) are stories a
  reviewer gave a specific category**. By guard: 28 scored nothing at all
  (0 recoverable), 29 fell below `MIN_PRIMARY_SCORE` (**18** would have been
  right), 10 were exact ties (4 would have been right).
- **A fix was built, measured and REFUTED.** Four relaxations of the
  ambiguity guard were simulated against the truth set. Every one buys ~2
  points of exact accuracy and **roughly doubles wrong-specific errors,
  5.4% -> 9.3-11.2%**. Wrong trade: `general` only withholds a story, while a
  wrong specific verdict actively publishes it onto an indexable category
  page. Keyword mining was also rejected — it returns one-news-cycle proper
  nouns ("uss abraham lincoln", "selena gomez") that would overfit the
  snapshot. **Nothing was shipped, deliberately.**
- **Severity re-measured:** all nine live `General` stories checked against
  all 14 indexable section pages — **7 of 9 appear on no indexable category
  page**. The 2 that do are rescued by `CategoryResult.all` feeding the
  `related` rail.
- **New finding:** that rail means an ambiguity-*rejected* category still
  reaches an indexable page — the Dolly Parton **philanthropy** story is
  listed on **`/health` and `/science`**. The guard protects `articleSection`
  while leaking onto the pages it was meant to protect. Filed as the most
  promising thread on this item.

## 6. Backlog item 5 (meta descriptions) — re-measured, worse than recorded

40 story pages: **17 of 40 (43%) meta descriptions end mid-sentence** (the
backlog recorded 25%), and **34 of 40 (85%) titles exceed 60 characters**.

A clause-boundary fallback was built and evaluated against 49 real summaries
harvested from page JSON-LD (`NewsArticle.description` carries the full text
while the meta tag carries the clipped one). It changed **only 2 of 49** and
cost ~60 characters of snippet on both. **Not shipped** — 4% coverage for a
third of the snippet is not a CTR win and would have been dishonest to
present as one.

The real mechanism, newly identified: many clipped descriptions are an RSS
standfirst concatenated to the article body **with no punctuation between
them** ("…on alert Suwadi…", "…smaller grocers A New York City business group
sued…"), so the sentence splitter finds no boundary. That is a
summary-construction defect in clustering, not a formatter bug, and it
degrades visible page copy too. Filed with the reason the obvious heuristic
does not work.
