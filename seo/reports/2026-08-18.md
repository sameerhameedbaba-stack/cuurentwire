## 3. Shipped

Commit `d69ea93` — *Fix Google News sitemap window: filter on the emitted
publication_date*.

**The bug.** `lib/seo/news-sitemap.ts` decided whether to include a story from
`cluster.lastPublishedAt` (the latest *source coverage*), but emitted
`first_seen_at` / `firstPublishedAt` as `<news:publication_date>`. Those
diverge exactly when a story published days ago picks up fresh coverage. Live at
11:18Z, 9 of 307 entries carried a publication date older than 49h, the oldest
`2026-08-14T18:38:49.853Z` — about 65 hours. Google News rejects entries whose
publication_date falls outside the 2-day window.

**The fix.** The publication date is now resolved first, and both it and the
coverage timestamp must fall inside the window. Late-discovered stories (fresh
`first_seen_at`, older coverage) are still included, and an unparseable
publication date skips the entry rather than emitting invalid XML. Dropped
stories stay reachable through `sitemap.xml` and `archive-sitemap.xml` — only
the News fast lane is time-boxed. 5 new unit tests cover the divergent cases the
old fixtures never exercised.

**Also fixed:** `npx eslint .` was reporting 1398 errors / 21362 warnings on a
clean tree, all from `.next/` build output inside an agent git worktree at
`.claude/worktrees/upbeat-gauss-e0a28b/`. The ignore patterns were root-anchored
(`.next/**`); they are now `**/`-prefixed. `npx eslint .` reports zero problems,
so the pre-push lint gate means something again. The stale worktree itself was
left untouched.
