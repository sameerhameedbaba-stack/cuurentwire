## Top fixes shipped today (all gates green, deployed, verified live)

1. **Story dates made logically sound** — dateModified could precede
   datePublished in NewsArticle JSON-LD (live example found); now clamped in
   both JSON-LD and og:modified_time, unit-tested. Bad dates are a Google News
   eligibility risk.
2. **One canonical host + homepage canonical** — www.currentwire.us served the
   entire site as a duplicate; now 308s to the apex. The homepage (highest-value
   URL) had no canonical at all; now self-canonical with og:url.
3. **Ranks 26–100 made indexable** — /top-100 pages 2–4 canonicalized to page 1;
   now each page has its own canonical, title, and rank-correct ItemList.
4. **Archive sitemap + IndexNow** — permanent story URLs older than the newest
   200 were in no sitemap; /archive-sitemap.xml now lists every non-merged
   archived story. New story URLs are pinged to IndexNow (free, keyless) on
   first archive, so Bing learns about them within ~30 minutes of existence.
5. **E-E-A-T and discovery wiring** — Organization schema upgraded to
   NewsMediaOrganization referencing editorial-standards/corrections/news-desk/
   contact; NewsArticle carries publishingPrinciples + correctionsPolicy;
   max-image-preview:large sitewide (Discover large previews); llms.txt added;
   RSS feeds extended from 6 to all 11 sections and advertised via
   link rel=alternate.

Also shipped: apple-touch icon, theme-color, 404 page title, archived stories
keep their OG headline card, corrections-log section.

## Top items still open (see BACKLOG.md for the full list)

1. Story meta descriptions can concatenate dek+body into mashed text — fix in
   the summary pipeline with tests (SERP CTR).
2. No CDN caching of HTML — ISR would cut crawl cost and LCP floor sitewide;
   needs careful verification of archive/redirect paths.
3. Entity-extraction junk creates duplicate/nonsense topic pages
   (/topic/bay-giants, -ceo/-ceos variants) — crawl-budget waste.
4. Single-source story pages are thin (~25–100 words, partly verbatim) — render
   attributed per-source descriptions; the #1 strategic risk for an aggregator.
5. Hero image priority + /top-100 eager thumbnails (CWV polish).

## Automation now in place (runs with zero owner effort)

- **Continuous**: IndexNow pings new story URLs from the existing 30-min cron.
- **Daily 06:47 UTC**: `.github/workflows/seo-health.yml` re-verifies sitemaps,
  news-sitemap freshness, feeds, llms.txt, key file, story JSON-LD sanity,
  canonicals, 404 behavior, www redirect — fails loudly on regression (GitHub
  emails the owner). Runs on GitHub's servers regardless of the owner's machine.
- **Daily 10:02 local**: scheduled agent runs the daily loop (verify → crawl
  samples → ship top backlog item → report). Runs when the Claude Code app is
  open; a missed day runs on next launch.
- **Weekly Monday 10:33 local**: scheduled agent deep run — full re-score, CWV
  pass, competitor gaps, backlog re-prioritization, llms.txt refresh.
