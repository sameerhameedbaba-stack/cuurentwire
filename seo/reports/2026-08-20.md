## 3. What shipped — publisher image weight (backlog item 1)

Yesterday's write-up said this needed "a real decision, not a quick patch",
because CBS's signed thumbnail segment binds exactly one rendition and
`images.unoptimized` leaves no local resizing path. Both facts are still true.
The conclusion drawn from them was wrong, because **both facts are about our
side of the request**. The publishers' own CDNs take a delivery width, and
nobody had asked them.

Six candidate query shapes were tried against one URL per host. What answered:

| Host | Before | Lever | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| npr.brightspotcdn.com | **6,366 KB** 7559x5039 | `/resize/976x651!/quality/80/` | **84 KB** 976x651 |
| assets1.cbsnewsstatic.com | **4,085 KB** 4896x3264 | `?width=976&quality=80` | **200 KB** 976x651 |
| platform.theverge.com | 607 KB 3840x2160 | `&w=976` | 99 KB 976x549 |
| globalnews.ca | 160 KB 1920x1080 | `&w=976` | 75 KB 976x549 |
| static.politico.com | **4,944 KB** | none — all six shapes returned the identical 4,944,055 bytes | unchanged |

Every rule was checked on **bytes and decoded pixel dimensions**, not status
codes: a CDN that ignores an unknown parameter returns 200 with the original
image, which looks like success in a naive check.

### The rules' limits, stated plainly

- An existing width is the publisher's choice and is **kept** — The Hill ships
  `?w=900`, and forcing our 976 would fetch *more* bytes.
- CBS thumbnails at or above 400 px are served **exactly as signed**; `?width=`
  on a signed rendition is unverified.
- `.bmp` / `.tif` are dropped at ingest and the story renders with no image.
  `globalnews.ca/.../Classroom.bmp` was live on `/health` at **6,221 KB** and
  no WordPress endpoint resizes that format. Side effect, stated honestly:
  `score.ts` adds 0.35 for having an image, so such a story ranks a little
  lower.
- **Archived story pages keep their pre-fix image URLs**, because normalization
  runs at ingest. Only re-ingested stories get capped URLs. The ranked and
  section surfaces — where the LCP lives — picked the new URLs up within about
  two minutes of deploy.

### The new standing check

`scripts/seo-health.mjs` gains `publisher image weight`, which measures the
first 15 publisher images on `/top-100` and:

- **fails** when a host we cap returns over 500 KB — the signature of a rewrite
  rule that silently stopped matching;
- **fails** when an undeliverable format is served — the ingest drop regressing;
- **reports, never fails,** oversized images from hosts with no lever, because
  a gate nobody can turn green is a gate everyone learns to ignore.

Verified live after deploy: `ok publisher image weight — 15 images, 1054 KB
total, median 70 KB, max 121 KB`, with 20 of 20 checks passing. Run
pre-deploy it correctly failed with `6217 KB` from NPR, so the check is known
to detect the condition rather than assumed to.

Guards: `tests/unit/image-upgrade.test.ts` — 12 tests, each rule carrying its
live-verified bytes and pixel dimensions in a comment so the next person does
not have to re-derive them, plus the negative results (Politico, Guardian
signatures, unknown hosts).
